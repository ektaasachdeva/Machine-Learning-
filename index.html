Problem Statement & SDG Alignment

Automated income prediction systems risk codifying historical societal wage discrimination. While standard machine learning models achieve ~85% accuracy, they exhibit severe Disparate Impact (0.31) and penalize women with higher False Negative Rates (43.1% vs 36.8%).

Key Research Insight: Blinding models by removing 'sex' fails (DI remains ~0.32) because proxy features like 'relationship: Husband/Wife' encode gender. Kamiran & Calders sample re-weighting successfully elevates Disparate Impact to 0.725 with <1.2% accuracy cost.
